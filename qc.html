Engineer decision sequence

1 · Before work / before hiding

Verify source, identity, dimensions, approved mix/JMF/drawing, readiness and sampling need. Do not let the next layer or concrete hide an unresolved issue.

2 · During execution

Measure objective process variables: layer thickness, moisture/density, temperature, slump/workability, rate of spread, cube sampling, rebar spacing/cover, truck/load identity.

3 · After work

Check finished dimensions/performance and link the evidence to the work location, chainage/member and relevant test report.

4 · If doubtful

Increase random checks, isolate the affected lot/location, preserve evidence, obtain the specified formal test, and avoid acceptance from a screening-only tool.

5 · If failed / nonconforming

Stop or hold the next dependent operation as appropriate, document the result, follow project/specification nonconformance procedure and seek the competent design/authority decision where structural significance exists.

  • Location / chainage / member identified
  • One objective dimension or process variable measured
  • Material source / grade / lot noted where relevant
  • Sample ID and date recorded where testing is required
  • Photos / GPS evidence captured where useful
  • Action / hold / pass status written
