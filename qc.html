Pavement concrete · sampling provisions to reconcile

Clause 903.5.2.1 (printed pp.430–431): The body text describes a minimum three-beam/three-cube sampling requirement per 150 m³ (or part), along with distinct early-age and 28-day tests.

Clause 903.5.2.2 (printed p.432): The initial daily beam-and-cube programme is higher and may be reduced only following the stated results and Engineer's decision.

Table 900-6 (printed p.436): The summary uses two beams and two cubes per 150 m³ (or part), with a separate daily minimum. Its specimen basis is not phrased the same way as the detailed clauses.

ENGINEER REVIEW / NO AUTO COUNT: These statements must be reconciled with the applicable contract, errata and approved QA plan. The app must not pick the smaller figure or issue a compliant/non-compliant sampling verdict automatically.
