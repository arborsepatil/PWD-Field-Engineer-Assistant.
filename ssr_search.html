How private SSR search works

Select your authorised SSR_2022-23.xlsx once. This app reads and searches the spreadsheet entirely on this device and saves an offline index privately in app storage. It does not upload the workbook, publish it in the APK, or sync it to other devices. The PDF stays accessible separately through the Library.
