Standards basis — current vs legacy

What this engine uses
Active: IS 10262:2019 for ordinary/standard concrete proportioning and IS 456:2000 durability requirements with current Amendment No. 6 considered. BIS lists IS 10262:2019 as reviewed/reaffirmed in November 2023 with no amendments.
Legacy only: IS 10262:2009 is superseded by the 2019 second revision. Its coarse-aggregate table differs from 2019 for 10 mm and 40 mm aggregate; this calculator does not mix the legacy values into the active engine.

IS 10262:2009 is legacy/superseded and is not used for active 2019 table values. Specialized procedures (M65+, SCC, mass concrete, air-entrained/special concrete) remain blocked.
Engineering status: preliminary proportioning tool. V1.2 supports user-specified SCM splitting within the ordinary/standard procedure but does not invent replacement percentages.
