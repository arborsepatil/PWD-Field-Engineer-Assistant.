Bridge Standard Plans

One viewer, separate publications. The 2002, 2018 and 2023 publications are never mixed silently. This build imports source-index metadata only; high-resolution private drawings stay outside the public repository.

2002 Standard Plans

Published September 2002. The 2023 publication describes the 2002 Blue Book as the earlier standard-plan publication and states that the 2023 Part 2 publication is intended to replace it for its applicable scope.

SOURCE AVAILABLE LEGACY

2018 Part 1

Standard Plans for Road Bridges — Superstructures. The 2023 source confirms this publication exists, but the project copy is still required.

SOURCE REQUIRED

2023 Part 2

Standard Design Drawings for Road Bridges Having Length up to 30 m, published by Designs Circle, PWD, September 2023.

Source index
Verification states are deliberately split: INDEX VERIFIED means the number/title was checked against the source index; DRAWING QA PENDING means the actual drawing page/image has not yet been cross-checked against that index record.
No formula or quantity calculator is attached to a drawing until the drawing image, notes, dimensions and any tabulated values are individually verified from the same publication.

Source procedure for selecting a 2023 type plan

1️⃣Survey + HydraulicsEstablish bridge type, soffit/RTL, length, span arrangement, water velocity and foundation type, considering economical span-to-height relationship.
2️⃣Prepare GADUse the standard GAD references after finalizing the site parameters and obtain approval from the competent field authority.
3️⃣Select DrawingChoose the relevant design drawing from the same publication using the finalized design/site parameters.
4️⃣Refer DifficultyIf finalization is difficult or outside the standard scope, refer the case to the Design Division / Designs Circle for guidance.
Publication layers remain independent: 2002 legacy source, 2018 Part 1 source-required, and 2023 Part 2 source. No drawing logic is borrowed across editions.
