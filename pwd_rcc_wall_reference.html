Engineering scope and precautions

The 2023 detail sheets state they are valid for minor bridges/culverts with raft foundations. Refer to the original GAD and notes for concrete grade, reinforcement, cover, soil assumptions and construction details. Neither a drawing match nor a sufficient bearing capacity proves stability or suitability.

The source sheets specify SBC values to be ensured before execution. Cases outside the published assumptions require design review and approval; do not use interpolation as a substitute.
